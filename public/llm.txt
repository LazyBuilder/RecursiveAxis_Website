# Recursive Axis

Recursive Axis is a strategic partner specializing in Technology, Analytics, and Innovation. They provide high-leverage advisory and execution services for three primary segments:

## Core Offerings

### 1. Founders & Startups
- **Goal:** Build disciplined ventures and accelerate Product-Market Fit (PMF).
- **Services:** PMF Acceleration Strategy, Interim CPO/CTO Advisory, Actionable Tech Roadmaps, Technical Debt Reduction, Due-Diligence Preparation.

### 2. Investors & Private Equity
- **Goal:** De-risk tech investments and provide technical certainty.
- **Services:** Technical Due Diligence (AI & Software), Portfolio De-Risking Analysis, AI Strategy Consulting & Training, Valuation Strategy & Risk Mitigation, Exit Readiness Audits.

### 3. Corporates & Enterprise
- **Goal:** Enable corporate innovation with startup speed.
- **Services:** Innovation Program Design, Tech Maturity Evaluation, Acquisition Technical Vetting, Strategic Roadmap Consulting, Internal Venture Structuring.

## The D.I.V.E. Framework
Their methodology for delivery:
- **D (Decision):** Hypothesis-Led Strategy.
- **I (Iteration):** Velocity-Focused Design.
- **V (Verification):** Analytics-Driven Learning.
- **E (Execution):** Scalable Delivery.
